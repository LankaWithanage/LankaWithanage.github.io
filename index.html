events-picker

events-picker is a private, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Privacy policy

The app uses Google Calendar access only to add events chosen by its owner to the owner's own calendar, and to check that calendar for duplicates.

No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data stays on the owner's own equipment.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
